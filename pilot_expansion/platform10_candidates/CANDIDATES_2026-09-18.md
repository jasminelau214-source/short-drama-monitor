## Decision framing

The evidence supports four different reasons for a Platform 10 slot, so a single “largest app wins” rule would lose information:

1. **Scale / ecosystem coverage:** PineDrama or Melolo.
2. **Balanced content research + public data richness:** StoryReel or My Drama.
3. **AI drama / animation intelligence:** VibeShort.
4. **Regional expansion into India:** Kuku TV or Story TV.

No option is locked. The next action is user selection; only after selection should a Platform 10 technical Collector test begin.
