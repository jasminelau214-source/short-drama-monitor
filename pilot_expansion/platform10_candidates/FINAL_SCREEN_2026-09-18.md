## Screening method

This terminal screen focuses only on five finalists:

- PineDrama
- Melolo
- StoryReel
- VibeShort
- My Drama

Three primary questions:

1. **Data value** — market relevance, content intelligence, field richness, regional value, and whether the platform adds a new research dimension.
2. **Overlap with the current monitoring pool** — separated into exact-title overlap, trope/mechanism overlap, and production-format overlap.
3. **Actual automation difficulty** — not merely whether a website can be scraped, but whether an official source exposes a semantically valid ranking compatible with the current Top10 contract.

Scales below are directional only and are **not combined into a single winner score**.

- Data / incremental value: 1 low → 5 high
- Overlap: 1 low → 5 high
- Automation difficulty / human cost: 1 low → 5 high
- Ranking-source usability: 1 weak → 5 strong

## Portfolio-coverage gap in the current 9-platform pool

A new dimension materially changes the interpretation of the shortlist.

Using Short Drama Data's 2026-09-18 third-party market snapshot only as a `DATA_MONITORING` signal:

- Current core pool already covers paid-app ranks **#1 DramaBox, #2 ReelShort, #3 NetShort, #4 DramaWave, #7 GoodShort, #8 ShortMax**.
- The two missing apps inside that paid Top8 block are **#5 StoryReel** and **#6 VibeShort**.
- Current core pool already has the free-app leader **#1 FreeReels**.
- **PineDrama #2** and **Melolo #3** would deepen free/IAA/discovery-model coverage rather than fill a paid Top8 gap.

Therefore Platform 10 has two different portfolio roles:

1. **Paid-head completeness:** StoryReel or VibeShort.
2. **Free-model breadth / ecosystem diversification:** PineDrama or Melolo.

This does not decide the platform, but it prevents a pure single-app scale metric from obscuring portfolio coverage.

## Overlap audit

### A. Direct-title overlap

Compared with the current 2026-09-18 eight-platform Pilot lists:

- Melolo official-web visible sample checked: 3 titles — **0 direct matches**
- StoryReel current `Most Popular` visible sample checked: 18 titles — **0 direct matches**
- VibeShort current public catalog sample checked: 24 titles — **0 direct matches**
- My Drama current explicit `Top Picks In` sample checked: 9 titles — **0 direct matches**
- PineDrama: official Web does not expose a public catalog, so same-method direct-title checking is **not available**

This only means the sampled titles are not identical. It must **not** be interpreted as low content overlap.

### B. Trope/mechanism overlap

**PineDrama — medium-high/high overlap**
- Official product emphasizes a general Discover library plus Trending and personalized recommendations.
- Public coverage describes romance, family, thriller and other mainstream short-drama genres.
- Strategic novelty is TikTok ecosystem/distribution, not a clearly unique story-formula library.

**Melolo — high overlap**
- Official store copy centers romance, CEO, revenge, contract marriage, family/baby, fantasy/werewolf, thriller, campus and historical.
- These are already heavily represented in DramaBox, ReelShort, NetShort, GoodShort, MoboReels, ShortMax and DramaWave.
- Incremental value is scale + Southeast Asia / IAA/free-model audience, rather than new story grammar.

**StoryReel — medium-high overlap**
- Most Popular includes mafia marriage, hidden identity, rebirth, alpha/serpent, second chance, revenge.
- Male-oriented pages add hidden tycoon, beggar/identity reversal, racing god, bodyguard, dynasty/time-travel and similar formulas.
- This adds useful male/female breadth, but many mechanisms are already visible in NetShort/DramaBox/ShortMax.

**VibeShort — high trope overlap but low production-format overlap**
- Romance, CEO, revenge, mafia, werewolf, rebirth, hidden identity and similar formulas remain common.
- The major incremental dimension is production: AI short drama / AI comic / AI-generated visuals / interactive-comic style.
- Public catalog taxonomy is unusually rich, including mutation, apocalypse, cultivation, xianxia, sci-fi, gaming, beastfolk, war god, supernatural and more.
- Therefore it is highly valuable to AI-content intelligence even when plot formulas overlap.

**My Drama — medium overlap**
- Still contains billionaire, mafia, werewolf and forbidden-love staples.
- But current public catalog adds stronger Western-original differentiation: sports romance, LGBTQ+, age gap, thriller, bodyguard, royal, school, espionage, cult/celebrity, poker/gambling plot settings, etc.
- It offers more useful differentiation for Western-original script development than another broad generalist catalog.

## Actual collection-path audit

### PineDrama

**Official evidence**
- Official website is a marketing/download page only.
- TikTok Help confirms:
  - Home feed = personalized recommendation
  - Discover = drama library, filters/tags, categories and trending content
  - TikTok/PineDrama accounts are linked for login

**Implication**
- Home feed is explicitly unsuitable as a ranking source.
- `Discover > Trending` is a valid content-shelf target, but current public evidence does not show explicit numeric ranks.
- A serious technical test would likely need app automation or discovery of an authenticated app endpoint.

**Cost**
- Highest engineering and account-maintenance burden among the five.
- Region/account state can affect output.
- High risk of confusing recommendation order with ranking if implementation is careless.

### Melolo

**Official evidence**
- Public Web is accessible and lists a small set of featured dramas.
- Official Web/store explicitly describes personalized recommendations.
- No explicit ranked Web chart was found.

**Implication**
- Public Web is useful for catalog/brand evidence, not a verified Top10 ranking.
- A true ranking path would likely require app-side endpoint discovery or automation.

**Cost**
- Medium-high.
- Lower account/platform complexity than PineDrama, but still poor fit to the current exact Top10 contract without further technical work.

### StoryReel

**Official evidence**
- Public Web is fully crawlable without login.
- Home exposes `Most Popular`.
- Genre/category pages contain large ordered catalogs.
- Title pages expose synopsis, tags, episode information and numeric counters/heat-like fields.

**Implication**
- Very strong structured content source.
- `Most Popular` currently has no explicit rank numbers; classify as `ORDERED_SHELF`, not Ranking.

**Cost**
- Very low to collect a stable ordered shelf.
- Medium-high only if the system insists on a semantically explicit Top10 ranking.

### VibeShort

**Official evidence**
- Public Web is fully crawlable without login.
- Home exposes `Most Popular`.
- All Dramas has pagination and an unusually deep genre/trope taxonomy.
- Drama pages expose synopsis, tags, episode links and two numeric counters.

**Implication**
- Strongest of the five for AI-content corpus construction.
- `Most Popular` is still an ordered shelf without explicit rank numbers.

**Cost**
- Very low for catalog/shelf monitoring.
- Medium-high for strict Ranking verification.

### My Drama

**Official evidence**
- Public Web is fully crawlable without login.
- Current page exposes multiple sections: VIP Series, Top Picks In, New Releases, Must-Watch, Most Trending, Best Choices, etc.
- `Top Picks In` currently exposes explicit numeric positions **1 through 9**, plus title tags and heat counters.
- `Most Trending` is rich but does not expose explicit numeric ranking.

**Implication**
- It is the closest match to the current ranking contract.
- The correct current classification is **explicit Top9**, not Top10.
- Do not pad rank 10 from Most Trending or another shelf.

**Cost**
- Lowest engineering/human cost among the five for a real numbered list.
- Remaining problem is semantic scope: accept a Top9 target or continue app/endpoint discovery for rank 10.

## Strategic interpretation

### If Platform 10 is chosen primarily for market-scale coverage
- PineDrama and Melolo remain the strongest candidates.
- PineDrama adds TikTok ecosystem intelligence but has the highest automation complexity.
- Melolo adds massive SEA/free-model scale but the highest content-mechanism redundancy.

### If Platform 10 is chosen primarily for script/content intelligence
- VibeShort and My Drama add more new information per collected title.
- VibeShort is especially important for AI drama / AI comic / AI-generated visual-format tracking.
- My Drama is stronger for Western-original genre expansion and differentiated relationship/conflict structures.

### If Platform 10 is chosen primarily for operational reliability
- My Drama is currently closest to the existing ranked-list contract.
- StoryReel and VibeShort are easier Web sources overall, but only as `ORDERED_SHELF` under current public evidence.

### If Platform 10 must satisfy all three at once
No candidate currently satisfies all three simultaneously:

- PineDrama / Melolo: market scale strong, strict ranking automation weak.
- StoryReel: balanced data source, but ranking semantics weak.
- VibeShort: strongest incremental AI-content value, but ranking semantics weak.
- My Drama: best ranking semantics / lowest cost, but market scale is smaller and public explicit ranking stops at #9.
